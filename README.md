## Privacy Policy for Cup and Ball
Last Updated: 4/5/2025

Thank you for playing Cup and Ball! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our game.

1. Information We Collect
Cup and Ball does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data. The game is designed to be fully offline, and we do not track, transmit, or retain any information about players.

2. No Third-Party Data Collection
Since we do not gather any user data, we do not share any information with third parties, including advertisers or analytics services.

3. Children’s Privacy
Our game is suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of unintentionally gathering information from children.

4. Changes to This Policy
If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version here.
